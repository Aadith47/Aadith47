## 🚀 Who Am I?

It started with a simple question: *how can a computer learn from data?*

That question took me from a classroom at Government Engineering College Idukki to real internships, and now to building AI projects of my own. Today I am a fresher **AI/ML Engineer** from Kerala, India. I like to take an idea all the way: the data, the model, the API and a working app that people can use.

I am still learning every day, and I am looking for a team where I can keep building. 🌱

**Highlights**

- 🎓 **Education:** Information Technology, Government Engineering College Idukki
- 💼 **Experience:** MERN Stack with AI Intern at Hexinox, and Data Science Intern (current)
- 🧠 **Focus:** Machine Learning, LLMs, RAG and AI Agents
- 🛠️ **Build with:** Python, FastAPI, Streamlit and Docker
- 🎯 **Open to:** AI/ML Engineer and Software Engineer roles

### ✨ What I Have Built

- 🤖 **Agentic RAG assistant:** an AI agent that answers questions from documents. Built with FastAPI and Streamlit, and deployed.
- ⚙️ **Machine Sentinel:** a predictive maintenance app. It estimates the remaining life of machines, finds anomalies, and explains its results with SHAP.
